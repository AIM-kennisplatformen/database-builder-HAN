# HAN Knowledge Platform

This glossary defines shared terms for finding colleagues through connected HAN data.

## Language

**Expertise connection**:
A sourced association between a colleague and a topic, such as a recorded skill or participation in a related project.
A project association does not by itself establish that the colleague has a verified personal skill.

**Manual mapping**:
A human decision that resolves an ambiguous or unmatched link between a source record and a person, project, topic, or other platform entity.
